    I.

    What is Guild?

    A self-managed peer community, not a course.

    Guild Professional Networks is a home for self-managed peer communities of business leaders and professionals. We organize and facilitate professional networking, powered by an advanced AI technology platform.

    Members spend their time solving real problems together rather than trying to separate real value from the noise. You make connections that turn into real partnerships, avoid the expensive mistakes of experimenting in isolation, and learn in weeks what would take months alone.

    VI.

    How it works

    From sign-up to your first event.

    1. Sign up for free.

      No card, no sales call.

    2. Tell us about you.

      Fill out the form so we can understand your background, goals, and contributions to the community.

    3. Join a working group.

      Based on your industry, role, and goals, you'll be placed into a working group of 8–15 members.

    4. Attend the group events.

      Access the event recordings in case you missed one.

    5. Speak at our public events.

      Volunteer to speak when you're ready.
